🎯 Copy-Paste Prompts — Try These Today

  1. Resume: "Write a resume for a veteran with [X years] of military experience transitioning to [civilian job]. Translate military skills into civilian language."
  2. VA Benefits: "Explain VA disability compensation in plain English. What's the process to file a claim?"
  3. Start a Business: "I want to start a [type] business in [city, state]. Give me a simple plan with startup costs and first steps."
  4. Job Interview: "Give me 10 common interview questions for [job title] and help me prepare strong answers."
  5. Meal Planning: "Create a weekly meal plan for [number] people on a $[amount] budget with a shopping list."
  6. Letter Writing: "Help me write a professional letter to [recipient] about [topic]."

💡 Pro Tips — Get Better Answers

🎯
Be specific. "Help me write a resume for a project management role — I was an E-6 with 8 years in logistics" beats "help me write a resume."
🔄
Iterate. If the first answer isn't perfect, say "Make it shorter" or "More professional tone" or "Add more detail about X."
🧒
Ask it to simplify. "Explain this like I'm 10" works great for complex topics like VA benefits or tax rules.
⚠️
Verify everything. AI sounds confident even when wrong. Always double-check facts, numbers, legal info, and medical advice.

🔒 Safety — What NOT to Share with AI

🚫
Social Security numbers, passwords, PINs, financial account numbers, or medical records. Treat AI like a helpful stranger — not your doctor or lawyer.
🛡️
Trust but verify. AI sometimes "hallucinates" — invents facts, fake sources, or wrong numbers. Always confirm important info with official sources.
