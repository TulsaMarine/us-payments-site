Section: The Catch

What AI Can't Do

Every tool has limitations. Smart operators know them.

🤥 It Makes Stuff Up

AI "hallucinations" — it sounds 100% confident
while being 100% wrong.

  • It will cite fake court cases
  • It will invent statistics
  • It will give you wrong phone numbers

🛡️ Trust but verify. Reagan had it right.

🔒 Privacy & Security

🚫 NEVER put these into free AI tools:

  • Social Security numbers
  • Passwords or PINs
  • Medical records or diagnoses
  • Financial account numbers

Treat AI like a stranger at a coffee shop — helpful, but not your doctor.

🤝 Not a Replacement for This

AI can draft a letter.
It can't sit across from you at a Rally Point.

That's what Eagle OPS is for.
Human connection isn't optional — it's the mission.

AI is a tool, not a battle buddy.

🚗

You Still Drive

AI is your staff, not your CO.

  • You make the decisions
  • It handles the busywork
  • It's a force multiplier — not a replacement for judgment

The Marine makes the call. The AI does the paperwork.
